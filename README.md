## About

I build computer vision systems and take them from a research notebook to something that actually runs in production — training pipelines, evaluation, serving and monitoring.

- **Research** — At CEIA, I work on the **robustness and fairness of deployed vision models**: what happens to per-class accuracy when a detector meets fog, low light and other conditions it never saw during training.
- **Engineering** — As CTO at WiMilk, I lead the technical side of an agritech product: dairy telemetry, data pipelines and LLM-powered assistants.
- **Interests** — data pipelines, model evaluation, deployment, observability, and automating the training/inference loop.
